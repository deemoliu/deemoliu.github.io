About

I work on the systems that sit underneath everything else — the databases, streaming pipelines, and query engines that other teams build on without having to think about them. My career at Uber has moved steadily down the stack: real-time analytics, then observability, and now online storage.

A theme runs through all three. Each time, the problem was a landscape of fragmented, partly-overlapping systems that had grown organically and become expensive to operate. Each time, the work was to identify the architectural limit, build consensus across orgs on a unified direction, and then do the unglamorous migration work to actually get there — consolidating Uber's OLAP platforms onto Apache Pinot, moving business metrics off Elasticsearch, and rebuilding logging and metrics on a single real-time foundation.

Today I'm focused on Cassandra: upgrading a large multi-region fleet to 5.x, strengthening failover and durability guarantees, and preparing online storage for the demands of AI infrastructure. I'm also an Apache Pinot committer and served as release manager for Pinot 1.4.0.
